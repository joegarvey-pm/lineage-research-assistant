# Research Log

**Append-only. Every search gets a row — hits AND misses.**

A logged miss is worth as much as a find: it stops you searching the same
collection again in three months. But be precise about *which kind* of miss
it was, because they mean opposite things later:

| Kind | Means |
|---|---|
| **Settled absence** | Records are complete for that time and place; the person isn't in them. Draw a conclusion. |
| **Coverage gap** | The records don't exist or weren't digitised. Conclude nothing. Find another record class. |
| **Method error** | Wrong place, wrong spelling, wrong jurisdiction. Search again, correctly. |

Always state your denominator. "Searched the county" means nothing.
"Read all 163 pages" means something.
